// IMPORTANT RULES

CBSE Exam Day Protocols & Mandatory Guidelines

Directly adapted from Controller of Examinations circular Dr. Sanyam Bhardwaj.

1

Mandatory Reporting by 10:00 AM

All examinations commence strictly at 10:30 AM (IST). Center gates will be locked at 10:00 AM sharp. No candidate will be permitted inside the venue under any circumstance after 10:00 AM.

2

15-Minute Question Reading Time

Question papers will be distributed between 10:15 AM and 10:30 AM. Candidates must read through the instructions and check question print clarity before writing commences.

3

Strict Zero-Gadget Policy

No electronic items, smartwatches, smartphones, Bluetooth devices, calculators (unless explicitly authorized for CS/math with special needs), or papers are permitted inside.

4

Admit Card & School ID Card

Students must carry their original signed CBSE Admit Card along with their official School Identity Card. Regular students must be dressed in their proper complete school uniform.

5

Stationery in Transparent Pouch

Only blue or royal blue ballpoint/gel pens, pencils, erasers, and transparent scale kits placed inside a clear transparent pouch are permitted inside the examination room.

6

Practical & Internal Assessments

Practical examinations for Class 12 (including CS 083 / IP 065 project viva and Python lab records) are conducted starting January 2027 by external examiners appointed by the Board.

Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions regarding the 2027 CBSE Class 12 timetable? Find answers below.

When do CBSE Class 12 examinations begin and end in 2027? ▼

The exams begin on Tuesday, 16 February 2027 (starting with Physics) and conclude on Friday, 9 April 2027 (Retail & Web Application).

When is the CBSE Class 12 Computer Science (083) exam scheduled? ▼

Computer Science (083), Informatics Practices (065), and Information Technology (802) will all be held on Wednesday, March 31, 2027 from 10:30 AM to 01:30 PM (IST).

Why did CBSE release the date sheet 101 days in advance? ▼

Early release was enabled by the timely submission of List of Candidates (LOC) by affiliated schools. As stated in the circular, this helps students prepare methodically, minimizes exam stress, assists exam centers in infrastructure logistics, and ensures board exams finish in time for JEE and CUET UG entrance examinations.

What is the standard exam duration and reporting time? ▼

All exams start at 10:30 AM (IST). Most academic theory papers are of 3 hours duration (10:30 AM – 1:30 PM), while select skill and practical subjects are 2 hours (10:30 AM – 12:30 PM). Center gates close at 10:00 AM, and 15 minutes reading time is provided from 10:15 AM to 10:30 AM.
